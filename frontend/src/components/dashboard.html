How the Protocol Works

A7A5 is a ruble-pegged rebase stablecoin (1 A7A5 = 1 ₽). Fiat deposits earn overnight interest in Russian banks, and that yield is automatically distributed to all token holders daily via distributeInterest() — no staking or claiming required.

Shares vs Liquidity — The Rebase Mechanic
_totalSupply (shares) 14,204,118
_totalLiquidity (token balance) 14,872,440
getScaledAmount(amount) = amount × totalShares / totalLiquidity
getLiquidityAmount(shares) = shares × totalLiquidity / totalShares
→ As distributeInterest() increases _totalLiquidity, every holder's balance (in ₽) grows automatically.
